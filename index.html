Close-up · the blades

Up close, it's two kinds of fiber.

Tall face blades in mixed greens give the colour. A shorter, curled thatch underneath fills the gaps and helps the blades stand back up after feet and paws.

Exploded view · 5 layers

Five layers, pulled apart.

Blades, infill, perforated backing, weed barrier, compacted base. Most of the work sits under the part you see.

Tap Blueprint at the top to see the wire geometry. Every shape is drawn in code, with photoreal materials wrapped on top and no video.

Drainage · from the other side

Water goes down, not across.

The backing is perforated, and the rock base is graded and compacted under it. Rain and rinse water move down through the layers instead of sitting on top.
